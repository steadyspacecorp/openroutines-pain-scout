# Product

Tallyping monitors scheduled jobs and alerts you when expected check-ins do not arrive.
A job sends an HTTP ping; the service checks whether pings arrive on schedule.
Website: https://tallyping.example
Documentation: https://tallyping.example/docs/

Tallyping is a fictional product used only to demonstrate this template.
Its website and documentation links do not resolve.
Use the brief as supplied to explore the workflow, then replace it with your own product.

# Ideal customer

Developers, technical founders, and small operations teams responsible for recurring background jobs.
They run backups, report generation, data imports, or synchronization scripts and can add an HTTP request to the job.
Company size, budget, and buying authority are unknown unless explicitly stated.

# Problems we address

- Backups or scheduled imports stop running without anyone noticing.
- Someone manually checks timestamps or logs to confirm that a job ran.
- Failure emails do not detect a job that never starts.
- A team wants an alert when an expected job completion is late or missing.

# Strong signals

Firsthand descriptions of missed runs, delayed discovery, manual checks, or requests for scheduled-job monitoring.
A concrete incident or recurring workaround is stronger evidence than a general discussion of reliability.
People need not use the words cron monitoring to describe a relevant problem.

# Exclusions

Promotional posts and hypothetical complaints without firsthand experience.
Requests solely for website uptime checks, application performance metrics, or log aggregation.
Requests for a scheduler, automatic retries, job execution, or backup storage without a monitoring need.
A successful ping does not prove a backup is restorable or a report contains correct data.
Do not claim this product fixes failing jobs or validates their output.
